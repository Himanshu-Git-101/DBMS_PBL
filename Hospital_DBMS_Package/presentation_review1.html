Context & Objectives

Problem Statement & Project Objectives

Addressing operational bottlenecks in disconnected hospital recordkeeping systems

The Core Problem: Disconnected Silos
  • Schedule Collisions: Doctors double-booked for the exact same slot, causing 2+ hour patient wait times.
  • Fragmented Records: Medical history, diagnoses, and lab results scattered across departmental logs.
  • Bed Disputes: No real-time synchronization between discharge and ward bed allocation.
  • Billing Leakage: Manual invoicing fails to consolidate room charges, tests, and pharmacy.
The Project Objectives
  • Centralize Records: Unify all 13 clinical and administrative entities into a single relational DBMS.
  • Enforce Schema Rules: Guarantee zero appointment overlaps and 1-patient-per-bed at DDL level.
  • Normalize to 3NF: Eliminate insert, update, and deletion anomalies while ensuring lossless joins.
  • End-to-End Workflow: Provide a smooth continuum from booking to bed stay and bill settlement.
Scope & Access Control

System Scope & Target User Roles (RBAC)

Clear functional boundaries and role-based access control

Functional Scope (In-Scope Modules)
  • Outpatient Profiles: Patient demographics and weekly doctor shift rosters.
  • Appointment Engine: Collision-free consultation slot scheduling.
  • Clinical Encounters: Symptoms, notes, and ICD-10 diagnostic coding.
  • Digital Prescriptions & Tests: Normalized multi-item medication orders.
  • Inpatient Care: Ward bed inventory with 1-active-patient-per-bed lock.
  • Consolidated Invoicing: Itemized billing and multi-mode payments.
Target Users & Access Roles (RBAC)
  • Administrator: Configures departments, doctors, wards, fees, and audit logs.
  • Doctor / Clinician: Views queues, logs diagnoses, issues prescriptions & tests.
  • Patient Portal: Discovers doctors, books slots, accesses test reports and bills.
  • Receptionist: Registers walk-ins and checks in daily appointment queues.
  • Ward Nurse: Allocates vacant beds and logs admission/discharge dates.
  • Billing Cashier: Generates itemized invoices and processes payments.

Deliverable 3 • Relational Schema (Part 1 of 3)

Domain 1: Outpatient & Scheduling Tables

Departments, Doctors, Shift Schedules, Patients, and Collision-Free Appointments

📋 departments
PK dept_id SERIAL
FK head_doctor_id → doctors (NULLABLE)
Attributes: dept_name (UQ), building_block, floor_number
📋 doctors
PK doctor_id SERIAL
FK dept_id → departments
Attributes: first_name, last_name, specialization, license_no (UQ), phone, email
CHECK consultation_fee >= 0
📋 doctor_schedules
PK schedule_id SERIAL
FK doctor_id → doctors
Attributes: day_of_week, start_time, end_time, slot_duration_minutes, max_patients_quota
CHECK end_time > start_time
📋 patients
PK patient_id SERIAL
Attributes: first_name, last_name, date_of_birth, gender, blood_group, address, emergency_contact
UNIQUE phone, email
📋 appointments
PK appointment_id SERIAL
FK patient_id → patients, FK doctor_id → doctors
Attributes: appointment_date, start_time, end_time, status (BOOKED, COMPLETED, CANCELLED), reason
CRITICAL UQ (doctor_id, appointment_date, start_time)
Deliverable 3 • Relational Schema (Part 2 of 3)

Domain 2: Clinical Encounters & Diagnostics (3NF)

Consultations, ICD-10 Diagnoses, Multi-Item Prescriptions, and Lab Investigations

📋 consultations
PK consultation_id SERIAL
FK UQ appointment_id → appointments
FK doctor_id, patient_id
Attributes: consultation_date, symptoms, physical_exam, clinical_notes
📋 diagnoses
PK diagnosis_id SERIAL
FK consultation_id → consultations
Attributes: icd10_code, diagnosis_name
Classification: severity (MILD..CRITICAL), diagnosis_type
📋 lab_tests (Catalog)
PK test_id SERIAL
FK dept_id → departments
Attributes: test_name, test_code (UQ), sample_type
CHECK standard_cost >= 0, turnaround_hours > 0
📋 prescriptions
PK prescription_id SERIAL
FK consultation_id → consultations
Attributes: prescription_date, general_advice, created_at
📋 prescription_items (3NF)
PK item_id SERIAL
FK prescription_id → prescriptions (CASCADE)
Attributes: medicine_name, dosage, frequency
CHECK duration_days > 0
📋 test_orders (Bridge)
PK order_id SERIAL
FK consultation_id, test_id
Pipeline: status (ORDERED..COMPLETED)
Results: result_value, normal_range, doctor_comments
Deliverable 3 • Relational Schema (Part 3 of 3)

Domain 3: Inpatient Beds & Consolidated Billing

Wards, Bed Inventory, Inpatient Admissions, Itemized Invoices, and Payment Ledger

🛏️ wards
PK ward_id SERIAL
FK dept_id → departments
Attributes: ward_name (UQ), ward_type (GENERAL, ICU, PRIVATE), floor_number
CHECK capacity > 0
🛏️ beds
PK bed_id SERIAL
FK ward_id → wards
Attributes: bed_number, is_operational DEFAULT TRUE
UNIQUE (ward_id, bed_number) | daily_rate >= 0
🛏️ admissions
PK admission_id SERIAL
FK patient_id, bed_id, doctor_id
Timestamps: admission_date, discharge_date
CHECK discharge_date >= admission_date
PARTIAL UQ 1 active bed/patient
💳 bills (Consolidated Invoices)
PK bill_id SERIAL
FK patient_id, FK consultation_id (NULL), FK admission_id (NULL)
Financials: total_amount, discount_amount, tax_amount, net_payable, paid_amount
CHECK total_amount >= 0 AND paid_amount <= net_payable
💳 payments (Remittance Ledger)
PK payment_id SERIAL
FK bill_id → bills
Transaction: payment_timestamp, amount_paid, payment_method (CASH, CARD, UPI)
UNIQUE transaction_ref | CHECK amount_paid > 0
Integrity Enforcement

Enforcement of Critical Business Rules

Translating hospital operational rules into database DDL constraints

1. No Overlapping Appointments
A doctor cannot be booked twice for the same date and time slot.

CONSTRAINT uq_doctor_slot UNIQUE (doctor_id, appointment_date, start_time);
2. One Active Patient Per Bed
Physical impossibility of assigning multiple patients to the same bed.

CREATE UNIQUE INDEX ON admissions(bed_id) WHERE status = 'ACTIVE';
3. Valid Admission & Discharge Dates
Discharge timestamp cannot precede the admission timestamp.

CHECK (discharge_date IS NULL OR discharge_date >= admission_date);
4. Positive Charges & Payment Limits
Zero negative bills; cumulative payments cannot exceed net payable.

CHECK (total_amount >= 0 AND paid_amount <= net_payable AND amount_paid > 0);
